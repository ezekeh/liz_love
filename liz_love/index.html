Simple 5-Step Process

How preordering works

No stress, no confusing currency conversions, and no guesswork. Here is how we bring your order home.

China
Nigeria
  1. 1

    Send your item

    Send us a product link from any store (1688, Taobao, Alibaba, and more), a picture, or a description.

  2. 2

    Get a quotation

    Get a detailed quotation in Naira with clear product pricing and estimated shipping details.

  3. 3

    Approve & pay

    Approve the quotation and pay for the product cost so we can place your order immediately.

  4. 4

    Warehouse check

    We inspect it at our China warehouse, then you pay shipping once the exact weight is confirmed.

  5. 5

    Receive in Nigeria

    Pick up your package or get it delivered straight to your city or state in Nigeria.

Two-step payment, no blind estimates. Pay for the product once you approve the quote (step 3), then pay international shipping only after your parcel is weighed at our China warehouse (step 4). Shipping depends on weight, size and item type, and every charge is shared before you pay.
